## LinkedIn (company page, Wednesday 10:00 UK)

Laboratories analysing samples from clinical trials sit inside the MHRA's GCP inspection programme, and the regulator's inspection guidance changed again this fortnight.

Issue 2 of Sample Matters covers:

1. A new infringement notice on the MHRA GCP page, issued on 21 July 2026 over a clinical trial that began without regulatory authorisation or a favourable ethics opinion. The guidance expects direct access to every electronic system that makes up the trial master file.

2. A hospital laboratory that calculates each aliquot's destruction date from the legal retention period at registration, with a weekly job flagging expired tubes for disposal.

3. The NIH HuBMAP portal, which links 9,316 datasets back to 5,142 registered tissue samples through a provenance model, counting blocks and sections as samples.

We also cover questions to put to specimen suppliers, Nigeria's planned eight-centre cancer biobank network and UK Biobank's gradual reopening.

## Why this matters (for excerpting)

- The MHRA's GCP guidance names laboratories analysing clinical trial samples among the organisations that must comply, so its trial master file and dossier expectations reach sample teams as well as sponsors.
- The clinical laboratory at the Hôpital Européen Georges Pompidou (AP-HP) sets each aliquot's destruction date from the legal retention period at registration, a model to compare with how a collection tracks retention deadlines today.
- HuBMAP and a specimen supplier both treat provenance from donor to sample to derived data as the record that decides whether material can be trusted and reused.
