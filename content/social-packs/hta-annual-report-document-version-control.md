## LinkedIn (company page, Wednesday 10:00 UK)

The HTA's annual report and accounts for 2025 to 2026 now exists in more than one format, and that has a practical consequence for anyone who cites it.

On 20 October 2026 the HTA added a web accessible PDF and a Welsh translation to the GOV.UK page. In the first Sample Matters deep dive, we look at what that means for quality records.

Three points:

1. The original English PDF runs to 84 pages and the web accessible version to 82, so page references may not match across files.
2. Both English files share ISBN 978-1-5286-6260-4 and HC 460, so the identifier alone does not say which file a page number came from.
3. The page shows a first published date of 16 July 2026 and a last updated date of 20 October 2026, and the two mean different things in a citation.

The same discipline applies to sample records: the history and version matter as much as the item itself.

How does your team record which version of a regulator document it cites?
