Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Who we are

Pro-curo Software Limited ("Pro-curo", "we", "us", "our") is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 07510777, VAT number GB124 2717 38.

Registered office: 10 Roffye Court, Crawley Road, Horsham, West Sussex, RH12 4DT, United Kingdom.

You can reach us at info@pro-curo.com or on +44 (0)1403 473839.

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office as a data protection fee payer.

What this policy covers

This policy explains what we collect when you visit www.pro-curo.com, why we collect it, and what rights you have. It does not cover your use of the Pro-curo application itself at app.pro-curo.com, which is governed by the agreement between Pro-curo and your organisation.

Information we collect

Enquiries you send us

If you complete the form on our contact page, we collect the details you provide, which typically include your name, email address, organisation and your message. The form is delivered by MailerLite, who process this information on our behalf.

Rhys, our AI voice assistant

This site offers an optional AI voice assistant called Rhys. Rhys is not a person. Nothing happens until you choose to open it and grant your browser's microphone permission.

If you do start a conversation, your voice audio is sent to Fireflies.ai, our AI provider, which processes it in order to understand your question and reply. Fireflies.ai is based in the United States. Please do not share personal details, patient or donor information, or anything confidential with Rhys. If you would rather speak to a person, use our contact page instead.

Closing the assistant ends the session and releases your microphone.

Fireflies.ai states that it does not use personal information to train AI models, and that it contractually prohibits its own suppliers from doing so. It also states that its third-party processing vendors do not retain meeting content once processing is complete. We keep audio and transcripts from this assistant for 120 days, as set out under "How long we keep it" below.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used, for example which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. This uses cookies and collects your IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you view.

Server logs

Our hosting provider, Microsoft Azure, keeps standard technical logs of requests to the site. These are used to keep the service running and secure.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To answer your enquiry. Lawful basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • To run the Rhys voice assistant. Lawful basis: your consent, given when you choose to start a conversation and allow microphone access. You can withdraw it at any time by closing the assistant.
  • To measure how the site is used. We do not currently ask for your consent before setting analytics cookies. They are set when you arrive on the site. You can refuse or remove them at any time, and the Cookies section below explains how.
  • To keep the site secure and available. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in protecting our systems.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. This site uses Google Analytics cookies to measure usage. To be clear about what happens: these cookies are set when you arrive, and we do not currently show a consent banner or ask you first.

You can stop them in either of these ways:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Every major browser allows this, and you can do it for this site alone.
  • Install Google's official opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout, which stops Google Analytics on every site you visit.

Blocking these cookies does not affect any part of the site. Everything works normally without them.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with providers who process it on our behalf, under contract:

  • Fireflies AI Corp (United States): processes voice audio for the Rhys assistant, only if you use it.
  • Google LLC (United States): Google Analytics 4.
  • MailerLite Limited (Ireland): contact form delivery. MailerLite stores subscriber data in the European Union.
  • Microsoft Azure (United Kingdom, region UK South): website hosting.

We may also disclose information where we are required to do so by law.

Transfers outside the UK

Some of our providers process personal information outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by appropriate safeguards:

  • Fireflies AI Corp and Google LLC transfer to the United States under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Both are current participants in that framework.
  • MailerLite Limited is established in Ireland and stores data in the European Union, which the United Kingdom recognises as providing an adequate level of protection. No transfer to the United States is involved for customers in the United Kingdom.
  • Microsoft Azure hosts this site in the United Kingdom, so no international transfer is involved.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence, marketing list entries, and any audio or transcripts from the Rhys assistant for 120 days, after which they are deleted.

MailerLite states that if a subscriber account is deleted, the associated data is fully deleted or anonymised within 30 days, and that records needed to meet legal obligations are held for up to seven years from termination.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask us for a copy of your personal information, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise any of these, email info@pro-curo.com. We will respond within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
