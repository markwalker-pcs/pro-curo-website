Case Study · Pro-curo Software Ltd

Sample tracking for a small research group handling human samples

Ludwig Research Institute · Research institute

The challenge

A small research group collecting human samples under an HTA licence needed a traceable system with a full audit trail. Their storage was a mix of -80 freezers and liquid nitrogen vessels, and their consent documentation lived separately from the sample records it related to.

What was put in place

  • Pre-defined location structures were used to profile the -80 freezers and liquid nitrogen vessels to exact specifications: shelves, racks, trays and boxes.
  • The history function provides complete traceability of every sample from the moment it enters the system.
  • Flexible data input means consent PDFs attach directly to individual sample records, so the consent travels with the sample rather than sitting in a separate file.

The outcome

The group can evidence the full history of any sample under its HTA licence, with the consent record attached to the sample itself rather than cross-referenced from elsewhere.

Who this is relevant to

Small research groups holding human material under an HTA licence, where the compliance burden is the same as a large biobank but the team is a handful of people.
